Madu Hutan Lestari: Sustainable Forest Honey from Sumatra

Madu Hutan Lestari is a forest honey product originating from Sumatra Island, Indonesia, particularly Riau, Jambi, and West Sumatra. It is sustainably harvested by local farmers who have received training in sustainable harvesting and post-harvest processing.

Full Original Article & Background

Madu Hutan Lestari is a forest honey product originating from Sumatra Island, Indonesia, particularly Riau, Jambi, and West Sumatra. It is sustainably harvested by local farmers who have received training in sustainable harvesting and post-harvest processing.

The business started around 2015 after noticing that not all sustainably harvested honey was absorbed by the market. Jusupta Tarigan, who has connections with local honey farmers, began purchasing and reselling the honey in Bogor, West Java, by marketing it directly to colleagues and customers looking for authentic forest honey.

Since then, the business has maintained its direct-selling method through a purchase order (PO) system. Most of its customers are repeat buyers who are satisfied with the product's quality and fair pricing.

Madu Hutan Lestari continues to provide high-quality, sustainably harvested forest honey to customers who value authentic natural products.
